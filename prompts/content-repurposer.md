Content Repurposing Prompt

Use case:Turning a single long-form piece of content (like a blog or video transcript) into multiple social media posts.

The Prompt:
"I have a long-form piece of content below. Please repurpose it into:
1. One engaging LinkedIn post (professional tone, use line breaks).
2. One Twitter/X thread (5 tweets, punchy and actionable).
3. Three short ideas for Instagram Reels/TikToks based on the text.

Content: [PASTE CONTENT HERE]"
