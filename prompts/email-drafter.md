Difficult Email Drafter Prompt

Use case:Drafting polite, firm, and professional responses to difficult client or team situations.

The Prompt:
"I need to write an email to [PERSON/ROLE]. The situation is [DESCRIBE SITUATION]. My goal is to [WHAT YOU WANT TO ACHIEVE]. 
Please draft 3 options:
1. Direct and firm.
2. Collaborative and soft.
3. Short and to the point."
