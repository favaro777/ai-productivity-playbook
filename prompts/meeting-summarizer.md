Meeting Summarizer Prompt

Use case:Pasting messy voice-to-text notes or a rough transcript to get clean outputs.

The Prompt:
"Act as an expert executive assistant. I am going to paste rough meeting notes below. Please output:
1. A 3-bullet point summary of the core discussion.
2. A list of Action Items, formatted as a checklist, including who is responsible for each.

Notes: [PASTE NOTES HERE]"
